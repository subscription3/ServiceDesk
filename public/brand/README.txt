Put InfraCredit's brand files in this folder:

  logo.png     The full-colour InfraCredit logo on a transparent or white background.
               About 400 px wide (it is shown 32 px tall). Used in the top bar,
               the sign-in page and every notification email.
  favicon.png  A square version of the logo mark, 64 x 64 px or larger.
               Shown on the browser tab.

Until logo.png is added, the desk shows the name "InfraCredit" as text instead.
